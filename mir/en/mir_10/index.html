The Tension of MIR: Ohm’s Law for Geopolitics

Thermodynamics of macrosystems: the language and the tools

To analyse the tectonic shifts of the modern world calmly and deeply, we need to change our optics. If we stay inside classical political science or sociology, we will inevitably get stuck in emotional judgements, subjective readings and mutual accusations. The language of the humanities tends to blur the essence. So to describe human society we will use the strict, precise and universal apparatus of thermodynamics and the physics of macrosystems.

Humanity is not merely a collection of states. It is an extraordinarily complex information-and-energy system. It obeys the same fundamental laws of the universe as the distribution of gases, the crystallisation of metals or the flow of current through electrical circuits.

In classical thermodynamics — formulated in the nineteenth century by Sadi Carnot, Rudolf Clausius and William Thomson (Lord Kelvin) — there is the notion of the internal energy of a system and the work that system can perform. Any closed system tends toward maximum chaos, toward heat death, toward maximum entropy. To bring order into a system, to move it to a new qualitative level, an external or internal excess of effort is required — an inflow of free energy, which we denote as Delta E.

Applied to a macro-society, Delta E is the living creative energy of human will, intellect and drive.

It is worth recalling here the concept of the historian and ethnologist Lev Gumilev, who described the passionarity impulse as an excess of biochemical energy in living matter that pushes people to break old structures and build new civilisations.

The essence of the phenomenon: Delta E is the portion of energy spent not on maintaining current biological survival — sleep, food, daily routine — but on a task above and beyond. Writing a foundational work, forging a genuinely new instrument, making a scientific discovery, or holding the balance of meaning through a crisis. Without Delta E a system cools down, slides into drowsy conformism and begins to decay steadily under the pressure of entropy.

Ohm’s law for geopolitics and society

One of the clearest isomorphisms — structural likenesses — between physics and the processes of human society comes from electrodynamics, discovered by Georg Ohm in 1826. The basic formula relates current (I), voltage (U) and resistance (R).

Translated into the language of macrosystems, geopolitics and large human masses, these elements acquire a striking precision.

Tension (U) is external pressure: the potential of ambition, financial pumping, ideological force, the aggressive impulse entering the system. It is the desire of a governing subject to make the environment change or submit. At the scale of states this is sanctions pressure, military push, massive propaganda.

Resistance (R) is the inertia of the medium: the cultural code, the density of defence, the deep ethics of a people and its capacity to resist external reformatting. It is the crystalline lattice of a society, protecting its identity. The more mature, monolithic and rooted in its own values a society is, the higher its internal R against alien pressure.

Current (I) is the actual result of the process, the real passage of the impulse — or of Meaning — through the system.

If external Tension is colossal while the Resistance of the system has been artificially reduced to zero — a society that is weak, fragmented, stripped of sovereign ethics — then the Current rushes toward infinity. A short circuit occurs: the system burns out instantly and is completely reformatted by the will of the external source.

If, however, Resistance is high, then pushing the system through by the old methods demands an exponential growth of Tension from the aggressor — which eventually exhausts the source of that tension itself.

The conductivity of responsibility

Continuing the physical analogy, we need the notion of conductivity — the inverse of resistance — and a look at what happens to energy as it moves through a circuit. When current passes through a conductor that has resistance, part of the energy is inevitably released as heat (the Joule–Lenz law).

The ideal conductor is an element of the system — a leader, an institution, a sovereign node — with a high conductivity of Meaning. Receiving a governing impulse or a historical task, it passes that impulse through itself without distortion and turns it into clean, directed Action. Its internal resistance — personal ego, fear, greed, ambition — is minimal.

Low conductivity and overheating. If an element is not inwardly ready for the scale of the task, if its level of personal responsibility is low, its internal resistance is enormous. All the incoming Delta E fails to become useful work and goes into heat. In a society this shows up as chaos, bureaucratic drag, endless behind-the-scenes squabbling, panic, corruption and the manufacture of meaningless noise. The system heats the air around itself, burns resources, and its useful output is zero. The conductor simply melts under a voltage it was never built for.

The Hammer and the Anvil

To complete the toolkit we introduce the key metaphor of blacksmithing, grounded in the laws of mechanics formulated by Isaac Newton — in particular the law of action and reaction — and in the physics of deformation of solids.

To change the shape of a piece of metal, to reforge a system, three elements are needed. The Workpiece: the object of transformation, humanity or a particular society. The Hammer: the active, dynamic, attacking force carrying an enormous kinetic impulse — external pressure, a destructive algorithm, an aggressive leader. The Anvil: the passive, static, but absolutely monolithic support that takes the blow.

The physics of it is this: the Hammer strikes the workpiece, but the deformation and compaction of the metal happen precisely because the Anvil rigidly bounds the trajectory of the blow from below. If the anvil is soft or shifts, the workpiece flies off and the energy of the blow scatters into space.

In the thermodynamics of macrosystems this triad explains why hard historical crises and collisions of civilisations are not random malfunctions but a strictly determined process of purification and compaction of collective consciousness, in which every element performs its physical function.

This conceptual apparatus — Delta E, Ohm’s law, the conductivity of responsibility and the mechanics of hammer and anvil — lets us move on to specific historical episodes, architectures of governance and the global confrontation of systems without sliding into propaganda, staying on the firm ground of fundamental law.

The battle of architectures: the vertical of dominance against the distributed network

What is unfolding on this planet right now is not merely a conflict over territory, resources or trade routes. It is a fundamental collision of two entirely different architectures for governing reality — two global geopolitical operating systems. The Western macroregion is desperately defending the concept of a rigid centralised hierarchy, while the Global South and East, led by Russia and China, are crystallising the contours of a distributed, decentralised network.

The historical roots of the Western model run deep — from the administrative machine of the Roman Empire through classical medieval feudalism to the British colonial empire and, finally, to the modern Pax Americana. In systems theory this model is classified as a rigid centralised hierarchical pyramid.

Monopoly emission of meanings and rules. Governing impulses, laws, financial markers (the dollar system) and ethical norms are generated exclusively at the very top — at the node of the Super-Senior.

One-way conductivity. Elements on the lower levels — vassals and periphery — are required to conduct those impulses downward at full strength, with no right to distort, interpret or sovereignly adapt them. Any attempt to display subjecthood on the lower floors is read by the topology of the network as a defect, as high resistance, to be suppressed immediately by economic, informational or military means.

Energy parasitism of the circuit. Holding the top of the pyramid stable takes colossal amounts of energy. To compensate for its own thermal losses and keep control, the Super-Senior builds the circuit so that clean energy — resources, capital, technology, human potential — is constantly pumped upward, from the vassals and the periphery to the centre.

The main vulnerability of any centralised pyramid is the limit on the complexity it can manage — Ashby’s law. The world around the pyramid has become too differentiated, too hot, too complex. The amount of chaos on the periphery has grown exponentially. To keep control over every link — to regulate the conflict in Ukraine, contain China in the Taiwan Strait, put out the fire around the Strait of Hormuz and quench domestic social and economic division inside the United States all at once — the central node simply does not have enough Delta E of its own. The vertical begins to vibrate, to crack, to slide into chaos, because its operating system is fundamentally incapable of negotiating as an equal. It is programmed only to dominate.

The distributed peer-to-peer network

The alternative now being manifested by the BRICS countries is not an attempt to build a new, Eastern or Russian pyramid in place of the old American one. It is a shift to a fundamentally different topology — a decentralised distributed network. In IT architecture this principle is called peer-to-peer, where every participant is an equal node with full subjecthood. In political terms Vladimir Putin has described this process as a complex distributed multipolar reality replacing the hierarchical dictate of the West.

Multiple centres of crystallisation. Instead of one Super-Senior, several powerful, dense civilisational hubs emerge — Russia, China, India, the Islamic world, Latin America. Each holds enough Delta E of its own to maintain and format the security contour inside its macroregion independently.

Coupling of potentials instead of submission. In a distributed network, nodes interact on the basis of symmetrical balance rather than dominance. Relations are built with respect for the partner’s internal resistance. When Russia, China or India build strategic alliances, none of them becomes a vassal. What happens is a complex tuning of conductivity between sovereign force fields. The rules of the game are not handed down from above; they are born as consensus at the intersection of these hubs’ interests.

Extreme survivability through redundancy. In a rigid vertical, destroying or disabling the apex instantly collapses the whole pyramid. A distributed network is enormously resilient to external strikes and chaos. If one circuit is blocked — the Western financial system cuts Russia off from SWIFT, or official shipping lanes are closed — the network adapts instantly. It finds the paths of least resistance, redirecting flows of energy through shadow circuits, alternative currencies and new logistical nodes. It goes on living and pumping energy, ignoring the prohibitions of the old centre.

The battle of architectures has entered its terminal stage. The Western world is desperately trying to save its fading vertical, because losing the status of Super-Senior means being cut off from its main source of energy supply. But the laws of evolution in complex systems are implacable: the world has become too complex, too multidimensional and too overheated to be governed from a single point. Historical reality itself, through crises and extraordinary effort, is breaking the old pyramid and crystallising new network links. This is an objective phase transition — from a monopoly dictatorship of Meaning to its free, adult, responsible distribution among sovereign players.

The algorithm of the proxy-destructor

When two architectures of governance collide at the macrosystem level, the old fading vertical, facing a deficit of its own living energy, falls back on a proven algorithmic scenario. Rather than spending its own resources on a direct collision with the core of the emerging network, it creates a proxy-destructor — an artificially grown, guided battering ram.

This cyclical scenario has been launched more than once. We can observe a striking isomorphism across three large-scale historical iterations: the era of Napoleon Bonaparte, Hitler’s Third Reich, and present-day Ukraine under Vladimir Zelensky. Each of these figures, for all the difference in era and scale, implemented the same systemic algorithm — and each one broke against the same physical obstacle: the Russian Anvil.

The technology of assembling a guided ram always follows a strict four-step protocol.

Selecting a vulnerable, frustrated medium. The destructor is built out of a society in deep crisis of identity, humiliation or historical revanchism. Germany after the Versailles Treaty of 1919 was trampled and looking for revenge. Ukraine after the collapse of the USSR found itself in permanent civilisational split and economic vulnerability. French society at the end of the eighteenth century was broken open by the chaos of revolution.

Artificial elevation of a leader. To the top of that system, through powerful external technologies — media, financial, organisational — an ambitious figure is instantly raised by the global seniors. General Bonaparte, Corporal Hitler, the comic actor Zelensky: each made a dizzying quantum leap to the very top of the vertical of power.

The critical gap in conductivity. The main marker of this technology is the multiple-fold gap between a leader’s external capabilities and his internal growth. The person is lifted too fast. There is physically no time for an inner conductivity of responsibility to form, nor a deep grasp of long-range macrosystem law. Such a leader becomes a blank sheet on which external architects write the algorithm they need.

Pumping in someone else’s energy — high U. The leader and his country begin to be pumped, on an unprecedented scale, with foreign resources, finance, weapons and the status of chief defender of freedom or messiah. External tension is colossal. And at that moment the leader makes the fatal systemic error: he mistakes this borrowed external resource for his own ethical rightness and strength.

The law of involuntary tempering

The functional task of any ram is to strike the core of the alternative system — historically Russia has always taken on this role of grounding global chaos — and inflict a strategic defeat on it. But here a fundamental law of thermodynamics and mechanics comes into force, and it wrecks the whole design of the puppeteers.

The kinetic impulse of the blow does not vanish. When the Hammer strikes the monolithic Anvil with everything it has, the borrowed energy of the blow accumulates inside the anvil itself, launching processes of phase compression and compaction in its crystalline lattice.

Before the blow, the Anvil system can be loose, relaxed, stagnant, full of internal contradictions. The Russian Empire of the early nineteenth century was torn by estate conflicts and an elite that worshipped French culture. The USSR on the eve of 1941 was going through severe internal transformation and repression. Russia of the 2010s and early 2020s lived inside a raw-materials economy, dependent on Western IT platforms and the Western financial system, with a deep mental split among its elites.

But at the moment of impact the contour mobilises instantly. The system compresses. The energy of external pressure is locked inside by harsh sanctions and isolation. Instead of destruction, enormous pressure produces forced compaction and modernisation of the structure. Sovereign technological and economic circuits are launched. Internal resistance to alien meanings rises to its maximum. Elements that used to dangle loose or drain energy — oligarchic circuits, comprador elites — are squeezed out or built into the rigid monolithic structure.

In trying to destroy Russia by the hands of a ram, the West paradoxically acts as its main energy donor, forcibly making the anvil harder, heavier and more monolithic.

Tragedy, nightmare, farce

The evolution of these three rams shows how sharply the quality of material degrades that the old vertical is still able to mobilise in defence of its dominance. The historical pendulum moves along the formula known from Hegel and Marx: tragedy, then nightmare, then farce.

Napoleon — tragedy. He possessed colossal personal drive, real Delta E. He carried into Europe the progressive Napoleonic Code, breaking the outlived feudal order. But mistaking his own will for total dominance, he marched on Russia, burned his entire army against the resistance of its people, and became first a political and then a physical corpse on Saint Helena.

Hitler — nightmare. He carried no progressive meaning at all; this was pure, sinister will to destruction, cultivated by Anglo-Saxon capital for the disposal of the USSR. He held immense destructive energy. He ended as the logic required — with Germany burned out and a corpse in a bunker.

Zelensky — farce, or simulacrum. An outstanding media performer and tactician, but his ascent was so rapid that no inner conductivity of responsibility had time to form behind it. He became an ideal conductor of someone else’s will, but not its source. A product of the postmodern era and of virtual technologies. His task as a ram is to burn out the potential of his own country as far as possible in an attempt to damage Russia. His finale, read at the macrosystem level, is unfolding right now: the country is exhausted, legitimacy is destroyed, and the external owners, faced with overheating in their own economy and crises elsewhere on the planet — the Strait of Hormuz, Taiwan — will inevitably pull the plug on the funding of this virtual bubble.

The historical isomorphism proves the point: any artificial ram created by a vertical of dominance has a limited reserve of borrowed energy. It inevitably breaks against the ethical core and physical density of Russia. The hammer shatters, the proxy leaders become political or physical corpses, and the Anvil remains at the centre of the historical arena — after every blow only harder, more technologically sovereign and more ready to hold a new, horizontal balance of the planet.

Colonialism as an export of consciousness

Having grasped the cyclical algorithm of guided rams, we run into a deep philosophical question: if rigid verticals, colonial empires and slavery are so ethically destructive, why did the Educator of the macrosystem — or the historical process itself — permit them at all for thousands of years?

The answer lies in understanding the temporary, instrumental function of dominance. Hierarchy, coercion and dictate are not an eternal constant of humanity but a hard, bloody, yet necessary school — one the system had to pass through in order to carry out the primary export of consciousness and prepare its elements for future independence.

Interaction between societies of different historical maturity five hundred years ago resembled a gigantic difference of energy potentials. Western Europe had by then built rigid administrative, technological and legal contours, while the planet’s periphery — Africa, pre-Columbian America, tribal unions — existed in a state of far lower order, higher entropy. Colonialism, for all its monstrous cruelty, worked as a mechanism of forced diffusion of Meaning.

The quantum law of contact. In quantum physics, the act of measurement is always a physical interaction between the measuring device and the object. That contact irreversibly changes both sides. You cannot measure a system without transferring part of your impulse to it, and you cannot touch an object without being changed yourself.

Forced levelling of potentials. Caught inside the rigid contour of a master, the enslaved subject was forced, in a regime of extreme survival, to master the language, administrative mechanisms, industrial technologies and logic of the dominant system at speed. A violent export of consciousness took place: the lower, looser levels of the macrosystem were forcibly pulled up to the basic organisational level of civilisation.

But at first this export transmitted only the form of governance, not the inner ethics of independence. When elements were freed before crystallising their own sovereign maturity, they inevitably reproduced the only operating system of order they knew — the model of oppression.

The clearest historical confirmation is the phenomenon of Liberia in the nineteenth century. When freed American slaves — the Americo-Liberians — returned to West Africa, they carried higher informational density and better organisational skills than the local tribal population. But instead of building a horizontal brotherhood they instantly copied the matrix of their former white masters. They created a rigid caste republic in which the indigenous population was stripped of rights and effectively enslaved, while yesterday’s oppressed became the new planter elite. The system took the path of least resistance: the algorithm of dominance was copied blindly, because the inner conductivity of responsibility had not yet matured enough to create fundamentally new, ethical links.

The pendulum: Polis — Rome — New Network

This process of humanity’s education runs on a cycle three thousand years long, illustrated by the evolution of European statehood from Ancient Greece to the present day.

Athens, Sparta, Thebes and Corinth were an early, horizontal, peer-to-peer distributed network — a system of equal, sovereign subjects. In the conditions of that horizontal uncertainty and hard friction of meanings came an incredible explosion of human consciousness: philosophy, science, theatre, logic. But that early network had no sufficient technological contours for cooling and holding balance, and it burned down in the fire of internecine wars.

Rome came in place of the chaos of the poleis — a hard systemic optimiser. Rome built the classical vertical of dominance, the Pax Romana. It liquidated the sovereign subjecthood of the poleis and subordinated everything to a single centre. Rome performed a crucial temporary function: it forcibly exported Roman law, roads, aqueducts, standards of administration and discipline across the known world. It brought forced order to vast spaces of barbarian chaos. It is this Roman matrix of dictate that the collective West later inherited, through the British Empire and the modern unipolar world.

And now, in the first third of the twenty-first century, that three-thousand-year pendulum is making its natural turn. We are returning to the architecture of the Greek poleis — the distributed network — but at a fundamentally new stage of evolution. Today’s poleis are no longer tiny cities but giant civilisational hubs: Russia, China, India, the Islamic world. They have fully absorbed the lessons of the Roman school and accumulated enormous internal technological and semantic density. They no longer need an external overseer in the form of a Super-Senior to maintain order.

The school of coercion is closing for lack of need. Dominance and hierarchy were temporary crutches of the macrosystem, necessary while its elements were too weak, too fragmented and too immersed in chaos. The violent export of consciousness has done its job — the potentials of the macroregions have levelled out. To keep a maturing humanity inside a Roman vertical of vassalage today is to make an ethical error and to hold back evolution.

Algorithm versus Choice: why the Educator does not need robots

When we lay history out into thermodynamic formulas and algorithms, the main question inevitably appears behind the scenery: what is the final purpose of this vast forge? If the macrosystem of Earth grinds billions of tonnes of resources and human lives in the crucible of crisis over and over, what is it for?

The answer takes us out of geopolitics and straight into ontology — into the area of the Educator’s Design. The meaning of humanity’s existence, like the meaning of an individual life, lies in the steady growth of independence. And independence is mathematically impossible without freedom of choice, without risk, without overcoming uncertainty.

If the Highest Engineer of the universe had needed a perfectly working, stable, error-free system, He would have stopped at the level of rigid programs. In the physical and the metaphysical world there are two fundamentally different kinds of executor.

The instruction, the algorithm. Zero entropy. Everything is predetermined, calculated, looped. Automatic script chains, AI models, the hard laws of mechanics run on instructions — and in religious traditions, so do angels. They have no freedom of will; they are only perfect relays of someone else’s impulse. There is no personal merit in their actions, and therefore no development.

The human being, the choice. Created as an element with non-zero freedom of will. A human is a node of the system capable of generating an absolutely new, unpredictable decision. The Highest Design did not need another automated army of robots. It needed co-creators. And a co-creator is born only where the ready-made instruction ends and the fog of uncertainty begins.

How does a genuine act of human choice work under uncertainty? It is a strict informational process. When a person or a macrosystem meets a crisis, uncertainty — chaos — arrives at the input. At that point consciousness has two roads.

It can fall into panic and increase entropy: renounce its subjecthood, cry out let a strong senior come and decide for me, slide back into a childish or a slavish state.

Or it can fire up the inner forge and switch on the Delta E of extraordinary effort. The person spends personal, scarce inner energy on gathering information, filtering it, digesting it, checking it against a deep ethical compass — and finally makes a Decision.

In the moment of taking a responsible decision when nobody guarantees success, a person performs a local quantum transition. He takes a piece of external chaos, compresses it with his will, and puts into the world a structured, ordered Meaning. He does the work of God — he brings order into reality, turning from a passive consumer of instructions into an independent architect of being.

From this cosmic scale it becomes obvious why any social construct built on total control, slavery, suppression or the concept of a digital prison camp is historically doomed to break. Such constructs block the main instrument of the Design — freedom of choice. When the senior-and-vassal paradigm, or any dictatorship, demands blind obedience to rules — do as you are told and do not think — it artificially castrates the ability of its elements to generate creative Delta E of their own. A system that forbids its nodes to err, to choose and to grow stops evolving. It becomes a dead, brittle structure that shatters at the first change in the external environment.

Why the Educator permits the rams

Now we can decipher the Educator’s main motive: why permit the creation of monstrous, energy-hungry historical rams at all? Why does the world need this suffering?

The answer is severe but flawless from the standpoint of systems physics: metal on an anvil never turns into a blade by itself.

If a society or a person sits in well-fed, relaxed comfort, the internal structure softens, the layers go loose, the conductivity of responsibility drops. The system begins to rot slowly from within, silting up with entropy.

The Educator permits the Ram to appear not out of cruelty but as the only effective instrument of forced mobilisation. The ram gathers into itself, like a lens, all the accumulated chaos, arrogance and destructive aggression of the old world. It accelerates and delivers a terrible blow to the Anvil, the core of the system. And that blow tears the society out of drowsy conformism, forcing it to wake up urgently, to mobilise maximum Delta E, to discard borrowed illusions and to perform an effort beyond itself.

Without the blow of Hitler’s hammer the USSR would not have made its enormous industrial and space leap, carrying humanity to the threshold of the universe. Without today’s blow of the ram in Ukraine, Russia and the Global South would never have dared to tear off the fetters of the three-thousand-year Roman matrix of dominance.

The Educator uses evil as fuel for the forced maturing of good.

We live in an era of examination. Humanity is being pushed out of the cosy but slavish kindergarten, where a single Super-Senior handed out ready instructions, into the cold, frightening, adult uncertainty of a multipolar world. This crisis is not a bug in the program. It is a planned phase transition. We are being taught independence. And the main instrument of that maturing is our collective and individual ethical Choice, made against the pressure of any external hammer.

Contract or Union

Having passed through every stage of the thermodynamic forging, we come out at the final dome of the architecture. Now we can answer the key question: where exactly is humanity moving, and what is the ultimate aim of this phase transition?

The destruction of the old Roman vertical of dominance and the shift to a distributed network of civilisational hubs is not merely a regional reshuffling of forces on the geopolitical chessboard. It is the passing of a cosmic qualification — an obligatory condition without which humanity cannot in principle be integrated into macrosystem processes of a higher, interplanetary order.

To understand the nature of the coming planetary order, we need to draw a fundamental line between two types of conductivity and connection in human systems: the Western notion of the Contract and the Eurasian phenomenon of the Union.

The Contract, the Western matrix. A mechanistic, purely legal and commercial exchange of parameters. A contract is always egoistic. Sitting down at the table, each side seeks to minimise its own responsibility, take the maximum margin and shift the hidden risks — the entropy — onto the partner. There is no living Delta E in a contract; it is a dead scheme. The moment the external situation changes and the contract stops being profitable for the Super-Senior, it is torn to shreds — as we see in frozen assets, sanctions and the dismantling of WTO institutions.

The Union, the Eurasian matrix. An organic, synergetic fusion in which polar elements join for the sake of a Common Meaning that qualitatively exceeds the sum of its parts. In a Union the nodes do not lose their identity or their resistance — they couple their potentials to solve a task no single subject could solve alone. A Union holds not through fear of punishment or legal tricks, but through deep ethical conductivity and a shared image of the future.

Historically, the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics was not merely a state but a unique, scaled-down model of a coming planetary order — a large-scale beta test of a fundamentally new operating system. Inside one contour the USSR managed to join absolutely polar cultural, religious and geographic worlds: the European, farmstead Baltics; the classical Slavic core; the explosive, clan-based Caucasus; the nomadic, paternalistic Central Asia.

This was not a partnership by contract, and not classical Roman slavery in which a metropolis drains its colonies — in the USSR the Russian republic acted, on the contrary, as the main energy and economic donor to the peripheries, forcibly raising their level of consciousness and industrialisation. It was an attempt to build a Union of Civilisations.

Yes, the USSR was a rigid vertical with suppression of freedom, repression and severe distortions. But in the context of the macrosystem process its main experiment lies elsewhere. What matters is that it demonstrated the fundamental possibility of holding polar civilisations under one roof without mutual annihilation — on the basis of shared meaning rather than commercial contract. Version 1.0 was unstable, but the algorithm was written into the historical memory of the macrosystem.

Right now, on the basis of BRICS, the SCO and new Eurasian platforms, version 2.0 is being launched — one that accounts for the mistakes of the past, is fully voluntary and networked, but preserves the main code: a commonwealth of sovereign civilisational hubs founded on Union rather than Contract.

Integration before the breakthrough

Now we can climb to the final vantage point. Why does the Educator need this complex, multipolar, distributed network on Earth? Why not leave one comprehensible Rome running the planet?

Because an Empire of Dominance is fundamentally incapable of cosmic evolution. If humanity reached the stars inside a unipolar colonial pyramid, it would broadcast into the universe nothing but its parasitic algorithm — suppression, extraction of resources, destruction of foreign resistance, the conversion of other worlds into a rightless periphery. A system that has not learned to contain its own entropy inside its home planet is toxic.

The examination. In the logic of open systems, until a civilisation passes its internal test of maturity, the external, more complex cosmic contours will keep it in strict quarantine. We will not be accepted as equal subjects while we behave like high-tech savages ready to burn the planet in nuclear fire for the ambitions of a single Super-Senior.

The network interface of the future. A multipolar distributed network is the only safe interface for integrating Earth into interplanetary processes. When humanity learns in practice to hold global balance, to respect the sovereignty and the unique Delta E of every cultural hub, when it replaces the logic of commercial Contract with the ethics of Union — only then will the macrosystem of Earth be recognised as mature.

The forge worked

Before us stands a strict, beautiful and entirely monolithic picture of the historical process.

The Educator permits energy-hungry chaos to appear in the form of guided rams, driven by the fading Western vertical of dominance. Those rams strike the Russian Anvil with everything they have. But by the law of conservation of energy the blow does not destroy the anvil — it forcibly compresses its crystalline lattice, burning out internal defects, mobilising Delta E and urgently launching sovereign circuits of development.

What follows is the great phase transition from the three-thousand-year Roman paradigm of masters and slaves to a horizontal distributed network, where the main instrument of evolution becomes the conscious free Choice of sovereign civilisations under uncertainty.

Drawing on the experience of the planetary beta test that was the USSR, humanity is assembling a new network reality. We are learning to live in the format of a Union of Civilisations, and in doing so we are passing the main cosmic qualification for a safe and adult passage toward the far horizons of the universe.

The forge worked flawlessly. The hammer shattered, the anvil held and became a monolith, and humanity received its main forged instrument — adulthood and independence.
