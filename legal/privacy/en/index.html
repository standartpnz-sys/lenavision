Privacy Notice

Version int-1.0. Service: Lena, an AI companion.

1. Scope

This notice explains what personal data Lena collects, why, and what control you have over it.

2. What we collect

  • Your messenger account identifier and display name.
  • The messages you send: text, and voice messages together with their transcription.
  • Files, photos, audio and video you upload, and short descriptions of them.
  • Facts about you that you tell Lena and ask her to remember.
  • Your language, time zone and settings.
  • Your phone number, only if you choose to share it.
  • Technical records of when the service was used, for reliability and abuse prevention.

3. Why we process it

To run the service: to reply to you, to remember the context of your conversations, to keep your files searchable, to send reminders you asked for, to apply plan limits, and to keep the service secure.

4. Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by accepting the Terms of Use before the first conversation, and the performance of our agreement with you. You may withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete your data; this ends the service.

5. Where your data is stored

Please read this. Your data is stored and processed on servers located in the Russian Federation. The legal protections there may differ from those in your own country. By using the service you agree to this transfer and storage.

6. Automated processing

Lena replies using a language model. Your messages are processed automatically to produce answers and to update what she remembers about you. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you is made automatically.

7. Who else sees it

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. It is handled by: the messenger you use to reach Lena; our own AI infrastructure, which we run ourselves; and, if you pay, the payment provider, which receives only what a payment requires. Voice recognition and speech synthesis run on our own servers.

8. How long we keep it

While your account exists. If you ask for deletion, your data is removed within 30 days, except records we must keep to meet legal or accounting obligations.

9. Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, ask for corrections, ask for deletion, or withdraw consent. Write to Lena in the chat and ask her to pass your request to the team.

10. Children

The service is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

11. Security

Access to the data is restricted, connections are encrypted in transit, and sensitive fields such as phone numbers are stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

12. Changes

If this notice changes materially, you will be told in the chat before the change takes effect.

13. Contact

Write to Lena in the chat and ask her to pass your message to the team.
